Sound Visualiser

Play a track and a full-screen WebGL scene follows the music — energy, mood, and beat. Nothing leaves the browser.

  1. Press Play demo, or use a file / the microphone.
  2. The look follows the track. Keys 1 / 2 / 3 change Cinematic, Live, Acid.
  3. Hover the top-right corner, or press Esc, to stop.
